## False Leads Ruled Out

- A long list of completed labs is not credible until each claim maps to an artifact and command.
- Resume bullets are weak when they describe duties without scope, action, and impact.
- STAR stories are not credible without a decision, tradeoff, validation, or result.
- Screenshots and diagrams help only when they are public-safe and tied to a specific claim.
- Missing evidence should be named directly instead of hidden behind confident wording.
- Redaction is part of the artifact, not a cleanup task after publishing.
