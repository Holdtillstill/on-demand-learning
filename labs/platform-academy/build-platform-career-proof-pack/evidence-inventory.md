# Evidence Inventory

Candidate artifacts:

- Trace Service traffic to ready Pods: selector mismatch, EndpointSlices, cleanup.
- Terraform EKS plan review: replacement risk, public ingress, IAM blast radius.
- SLO-backed runbook: alert, rollout history, mitigation choices.
- Tenant boundary audit: RBAC, secret access, NetworkPolicy, Pod Security.
- Cost driver audit: over-requested workloads, idle load balancers, abandoned PVCs.

Missing proof to collect:

- Screenshots of lab output.
- Before/after manifest snippets.
- Short rollback notes.
- One architecture diagram.
- One measurable business or reliability outcome.
